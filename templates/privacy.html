Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Spendly ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our personal finance and expense tracking application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information to provide you with personal expense tracking features:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted credentials.
  • Expense & Transaction Data: Information you voluntarily record, such as transaction amounts, dates, expense categories, payment methods, and descriptions.
  • Usage & Device Data: Technical information such as your browser type, device details, and log data when you interact with Spendly, solely to ensure application reliability.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for purposes related to delivering personal financial insights to you:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide the core expense tracking and budgeting tools.
  • To generate spending reports, monthly summaries, and category breakdowns for your personal view.
  • To authenticate your identity, prevent fraud, and ensure account security.
  • To communicate with you regarding security updates, technical notices, and support inquiries.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal financial data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage

Your financial records are stored securely with appropriate administrative and technical safeguards:

  • Encryption: Passwords and sensitive data are hashed and protected using modern cryptographic standards.
  • Retention: We retain your expense data for as long as your account is active or needed to provide you with the Service. You can delete your transactions or account at any time.
  • Security Practices: We regularly monitor our systems for potential vulnerabilities to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your individual expense records with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Trusted cloud hosting and database service providers who adhere to strict data security standards and confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process to protect the rights, property, or safety of Spendly, our users, or the public.

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or behavioral analytics scripts that monitor your personal spending habits.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
